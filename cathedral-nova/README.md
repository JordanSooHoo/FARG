# Cathedral Nova

A keyboard-only rhythm shooter. You fly a pipe-organ starship through a space battle, firing on the melody and dodging enemy fire that lands on the beat. The core loop is inspired by the TI-84 calculator shooter *Phoenix*, with far more color.

**Play:** https://jordansoohoo.github.io/FARG/cathedral-nova/

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Arrows / WASD | Fly |
| Z X C (or J K L) | Fire the bass, tenor and treble pipes when a note reaches the line |
| Shift | Shield |
| Space | Cannon in D (clears bullets once the meter is full) |
| F | Fermata: slows enemy fire for 4 beats (classical only) |
| Esc | Pause |

On the song screen, ← → changes difficulty, `[` `]` adjusts audio offset and `-` `=` changes note speed.

## Music

Built-in songs are synthesized in the browser:

- *Toccata of the Void*: Bach, Toccata & Fugue in D minor (pipe organ)
- *Hall of the Asteroid King*: Grieg, In the Hall of the Mountain King (orchestral, accelerates like the original)
- *Ode to the Stars*: Beethoven, Symphony No. 9 (classical)
- *Nebula Drive* and *Pulsar Breaks*: original synthwave and drum & bass

You can also load your own audio file. The game estimates its tempo and charts bass, mid and treble hits into the three pipes.

## Cathedral Mode

Organ, orchestral and classical music unlock bonuses: a stained-glass sky, ×1.25 score and a faster-charging Cannon in D.

- **Pipe organ:** sustain beams on long notes, two-key chords, and extra pipes ("stops") at 25 / 50 / 100 combo
- **Orchestral:** string, brass and woodwind wingmen join at 20 / 40 / 60 combo
- **Classical:** wider timing windows and the Fermata

## A note on loops

The Toccata level ends with the boss *The Dissonance*, which only goes away when it's resolved. Hofstadter would probably call the rest a strange loop: a canon fired from a cannon, in D.
